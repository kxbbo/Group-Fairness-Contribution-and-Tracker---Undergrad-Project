From first task to final grade

  1. Plan the work

    Teams agree on a charter and split the project into weighted tasks.

  2. Show the proof

    Members upload evidence, log hours, and check in to meetings.

  3. Verify

    A teammate reviews the evidence before a task counts.

  4. Rate each other

    Peer rounds are anonymous and checked for unfair or copied ratings.

  5. Review and grade

    Instructors see the full score breakdown and decide any grade adjustment.

What FairShare covers

Teams and setup

  • Course and formula setupSet milestones, team sizes, and the weights of the fairness formula. Weights must total 100%.
  • Team formationInvite codes, size limits, instructor-picked leaders, transfer history, and a lock date.
  • Team charterRoles, meeting times, and a late-work policy that every member accepts.
  • Skill-based suggestionsBalanced teams suggested from skills and weekly availability.
  • Team pulseProgress, workload per member, milestones, and recent activity.

Tasks and workload

  • Weighted task boardTasks carry a difficulty from 1 to 5, with clear rules for who can move them.
  • Peer-verified completionA teammate checks the evidence before a task counts.
  • Workload advisorFlags members carrying the team and members with low contribution.
  • Shared-task splitsParticipants declare their percentages and teammates confirm them.
  • Handover creditReassigned tasks split credit between the old and new owner.

Evidence and analytics

  • Evidence vaultVersioned uploads with a SHA-256 hash, so changed files are flagged.
  • Time logsOverlap and daily-limit checks, leader approval, and plausibility flags.
  • Meetings and attendanceRotating check-in codes, minutes, and action items that become tasks.
  • Contribution analyticsEach member’s share, hours, attendance, and activity against the team average.
  • Last-minute rush detectorShows who worked steadily and who left it for the final stretch.

Peer evaluation and grading

  • Anonymous peer roundsRate teammates on effort, reliability, communication, and quality.
  • Rating integrity checkA Python service flags identical ratings and reciprocal inflation.
  • Score and what-if simulatorA full breakdown, plus weight changes you can test before saving.
  • Team health warningsOn Track, At Risk, or Critical, with the reasons listed.
  • Grading supportSuggested contribution factors, overrides with a written reason, private concerns, and PDF or CSV export.

One workspace, four roles

Student

Work on tasks, upload evidence, log time, rate teammates, and see your own score breakdown.

Team leader

Everything a student does, plus assigning tasks, approving time logs, and running meetings.

Instructor

Set up courses and formulas, watch team health, review rating flags, and decide grade adjustments.

Admin

Manage users and courses, assign instructors, and read the system logs.
